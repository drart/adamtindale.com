Untitled 8 is the first interface implemented using [JunctionBox](http://www.cirolab.org/blog/?attachment_id=967) by a programmer other than the primary author. The audio engine consists of a single frequency modulated oscillator with reverb and granular synthesis effects processing, all implemented in Pd. The interface is designed for the realization of a loosely scored composition.
