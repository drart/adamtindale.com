# Bitstance Project Information

- **Bandcamp:** https://bitstance.bandcamp.com/
- **Bitstance Radio:** https://bitstance.org/

**Bitstance** is a collaborative electronic music project that performs live, improvised, noise-adjacent electronic music using networked custom software and modular analog synthesizers. The group was formed at the start of the pandemic and consists of four members based in Toronto and Washington DC.
